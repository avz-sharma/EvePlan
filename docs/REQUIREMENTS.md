## Google Places policy decision

The Places policy allows Place IDs to be cached, but does not allow caching place display content. EvePlan therefore caches only IDs for 30 seconds and re-fetches visible search content instead of serving cached names or addresses. Search results are rendered with the Google map; manual/map-link mode displays no Places result content. Rating, phone, and website are fetched only from the explicit details action. Public Terms of Use and a Privacy Policy incorporating Google's terms must be published before enabling Places in a public deployment. See the [Phase 2 report](phase-2-report.md) for source links and the cost implication.
